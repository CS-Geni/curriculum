## Capstone: Production-ready service

Containerize, test, deploy, monitor, and document a small web service.

The brief, and the checklist for calling it done, is in [projects](https://github.com/CS-Geni/projects/blob/main/briefs/shipping-software.md).

- The service has a health check.
- A test runs before the artifact is published.
- You can tell from a log or metric whether the service is up.
- A short runbook says how to restart it and what to look at first.
