# Discrete Math for Builders

**Math for computer science** · Intermediate · 36 hours · 9 lessons

Learn the logic, proofs, counting, graphs, and probability that make computer science click—through code and concrete examples.

Open the [CS-Geni learning site](https://cs-geni.github.io/public/) and choose **Discrete Math for Builders**. Progress on that site stays in your browser.

## You will be able to

- Read and write simple proofs
- Use sets, logic, and relations
- Count possibilities systematically
- Model problems with graphs and probability

## Before you start

- Python: Your First Programs
- High-school algebra

## Modules

### Logic and proof

Make precise claims and support them.

1. Propositions
2. Implication
3. Proof by induction

### Count and relate

Sets, functions, relations, and combinatorics.

1. Set operations
2. Functions and relations
3. Counting without listing

### Graphs and chance

Model connections and uncertainty.

1. Graph vocabulary
2. Trees and connectivity
3. Probability foundations

## Capstone: Fair tournament scheduler

Use graphs and counting to generate and validate a tournament schedule.

The brief, and the checklist for calling it done, is in [projects](https://github.com/CS-Geni/projects/blob/main/briefs/discrete-math.md).

- Teams or players are vertices, and games are edges.
- The schedule gives every participant the matches you promised.
- A short counting argument shows the schedule is complete.
