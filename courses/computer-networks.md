# Networks from First Principles

**Networks & distributed systems** · Intermediate · 36 hours · 9 lessons

Follow data from your app to the wire and back while learning layers, protocols, routing, HTTP, and reliable communication.

Open the [CS-Geni learning site](https://cs-geni.github.io/public/) and choose **Networks from First Principles**. Progress on that site stays in your browser.

## You will be able to

- Explain the internet layer by layer
- Inspect HTTP and DNS traffic
- Build a socket-based service
- Reason about latency, reliability, and congestion

## Before you start

- Python: Your First Programs
- Inside a Computer recommended

DNS, HTTP, security, and sockets.

1. Names with DNS
2. HTTP conversations
3. Build with sockets

## Capstone: Resilient chat service

Build a multi-client chat protocol and test what happens under delay and disconnection.

The brief, and the checklist for calling it done, is in [projects](https://github.com/CS-Geni/projects/blob/main/briefs/computer-networks.md).

- Two clients can exchange a line of text through your service.
- You wrote down the messages in the protocol, including who sends first.
- You describe what a client does after a delay and after a disconnect.
