# Computer Science Without the Jargon

**Start from zero** · Beginner · 8 hours · 9 lessons

Learn what computers actually do, how programmers turn messy ideas into precise steps, and how to feel at home in a technical world.

Open the [CS-Geni learning site](https://cs-geni.github.io/public/) and choose **Computer Science Without the Jargon**. Progress on that site stays in your browser.

## You will be able to

- Explain how data and instructions work together
- Translate everyday tasks into algorithms
- Use files, folders, and a terminal with confidence
- Recognize the major fields of computer science

## Before you start

- Curiosity
- Comfort using a web browser

## Modules

### Meet the machine

A plain-language tour of hardware, software, data, and the internet.

1. What makes a computer a computer?
2. Bits: tiny choices, big ideas
3. Files, programs, and operating systems

### Think like a programmer

Practice precise instructions, patterns, and useful decomposition.

1. Algorithms in everyday life
2. Break a problem into pieces
3. When instructions go wrong

### Your working toolkit

Get comfortable with the tools learners and professionals use.

1. Navigate your computer
2. Your first terminal commands
3. How to learn from errors

## Capstone: Design a tiny digital assistant

Create a flowchart and precise instructions for a helpful everyday automation—no code required.

The brief, and the checklist for calling it done, is in [projects](https://github.com/CS-Geni/projects/blob/main/briefs/computer-thinking.md).

- A reader who was not in the room can follow the steps without asking a question.
- The flowchart has a start, an end, and at least one decision.
- Inputs and outputs are named before the steps.
